Privacy

Your collection stays on this device.

Collection and room data

This static release stores collection details, shelf measurements, settings, selling calculations and build sessions in your browser. SetRoom does not operate an account database or receive that information.

Camera and recordings

Camera access is requested only when you start a Build Replay recording. The live image and saved recording remain in the browser unless you deliberately download or share them.

External images and future checkout

Set images are requested from third-party image hosts, which receive ordinary web request information. A future payment or licensing provider will process its own checkout information under that provider’s privacy policy.

Backups

Browser storage can be cleared by the user, browser or device. Export a JSON backup before clearing site data or moving devices. Video replays should be downloaded separately.
